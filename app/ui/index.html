Choose the vault you want to understand

Property Studio reads your vault and never changes it. Nothing is uploaded, no account or API key is needed, and no note is created, renamed or edited. Everything you produce here is text that you copy into Obsidian yourself.

Tip on Windows: open the vault folder, click the address bar, copy the path. Sub-folders are scanned automatically. .obsidian/, .trash/ and hidden folders are ignored, and symlinks/junctions are never followed out of the vault.

Plan a property change safely

Every plan is analysis only. Property Studio v1 has no code path that writes to your vault — it shows you exactly which notes are affected, and where a human decision is required.

Relationship inbox

Property values that point at other notes — exact matches you could turn into links, broken links, ambiguous names and drift. Nothing is changed in your vault.

Property health check

A deterministic, explainable health score for your vault's property layer. Weight each finding, cap runaway deductions, and drill down to every affected note.

External AI / Agent proposal import

Import a schema proposal generated by an external agent (such as the Obsidian Property Architect Skill). Property Studio has no AI dependency of its own — it validates proposals locally, checks them against your vault, and lets you decide.
